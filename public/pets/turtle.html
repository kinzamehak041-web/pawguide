Turtle Care Guide

Long-lived reptiles needing UV light, a basking spot and clean water or a proper habitat.

DifficultyMedium-Hard
Lifespan25-80+ years
CostMedium-High
SizeSmall to large

🍽️ Food & Diet

  • Water turtles: turtle pellets plus leafy greens and occasional insects or fish.
  • Tortoises: mostly leafy greens, weeds and some vegetables; little fruit.
  • Provide calcium (cuttlebone) and keep a UVB light.

🏠 Housing

  • Aquatic turtles need a large tank (10 gallons per inch of shell) with filter and dry basking dock.
  • Tortoises need a spacious pen with hide and substrate for burrowing.
  • UVB lamp 10-12 hours daily; replace every 6-12 months.

📅 Daily Routine

  • Feed and refresh water.
  • Ensure basking heat 30-35 C and UVB working.

🛁 Grooming

  • Do not scrub the shell hard; a soft brush is enough.
  • Change tank water often.

🩺 Health & Vet Care

  • Soft shell, swollen eyes, runny nose or refusing food need a reptile vet.
  • Wash hands after handling; turtles can carry Salmonella.

🎓 Training

  • Turtles learn feeding routine; handle rarely.

⚠️ Foods & Things to Avoid

  • Dog or cat food
  • Iceberg lettuce
  • Cold rooms
  • Wild collection

💡 Pro Tips

  • Turtles can outlive you - plan long term.
  • Check local laws before buying.
