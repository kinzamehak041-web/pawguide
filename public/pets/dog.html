Dog Care Guide

Loyal, social companions that need daily exercise, training and lots of attention.

DifficultyMedium
Lifespan10-15 years
CostMedium-High
SizeSmall to giant depending on breed

🍽️ Food & Diet

  • Feed a complete dog food suited to age (puppy, adult, senior). Puppies eat 3-4 small meals a day, adults 2 meals.
  • Fresh clean water must always be available.
  • Cooked plain chicken, rice, boiled egg and carrots are safe occasional extras.
  • Treats should be under 10% of daily calories.

🏠 Housing

  • A quiet bed or crate in a warm, dry corner away from drafts.
  • Secure yard or leash walks; dogs must not be left tied all day.
  • Provide shade and cool water in summer; a warm blanket in winter.

📅 Daily Routine

  • 30-90 minutes of walking or play depending on breed.
  • Two feeding times and fresh water changes.
  • Short training sessions (5-10 minutes) and affection.

🛁 Grooming

  • Brush 1-3 times a week (daily for long coats).
  • Bath every 4-6 weeks with dog shampoo only.
  • Trim nails monthly and brush teeth 2-3 times a week.
  • Check ears weekly for dirt or bad smell.

🩺 Health & Vet Care

  • Vaccinate: puppy series, then yearly boosters (including rabies).
  • Deworm regularly and use tick/flea prevention.
  • Vet check-up once a year; twice a year for seniors.
  • Warning signs: vomiting more than once, not eating for a day, limping, bloated belly, heavy panting.

🎓 Training

  • Start at 8 weeks: sit, come, stay, leave it.
  • Use rewards and praise; never hit or shout.
  • Socialize puppies early with people, sounds and other dogs.
  • Teach toilet routine: out after waking, eating and playing.

⚠️ Foods & Things to Avoid

  • Chocolate
  • Grapes and raisins
  • Onion and garlic
  • Cooked bones
  • Xylitol (sweetener)
  • Spicy or oily human food

💡 Pro Tips

  • Never leave a dog in a closed car.
  • Microchip or ID tag helps if the dog gets lost.
  • Choose a breed that fits your home size and energy.
