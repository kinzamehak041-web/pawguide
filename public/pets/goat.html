Goat Care Guide

Hardy, curious farm animals that give milk and need dry shelter and good forage.

DifficultyMedium
Lifespan10-15 years
CostLow-Medium
SizeMedium

🍽️ Food & Diet

  • Good hay, browse (leaves, shrubs) and clean water.
  • Mineral supplement made for goats; grain in small amounts for milking or growing goats.
  • Introduce new feed slowly.

🏠 Housing

  • Dry, draft-free shelter and a strong fence.
  • Raised platforms to climb and rest.
  • Keep dry bedding; goats dislike mud and rain.

📅 Daily Routine

  • Grazing or browsing 6-8 hours, fresh water, health check by eye.
  • Milking twice a day for dairy goats.

🛁 Grooming

  • Trim hooves every 6-8 weeks.
  • Brush coat occasionally.

🩺 Health & Vet Care

  • Vaccinate (CD&T) and deworm per vet advice.
  • Watch for diarrhea, bloat, lameness and loss of appetite.
  • Ask a livestock vet about local diseases.

🎓 Training

  • Goats learn routines, leading and milking stand easily.

⚠️ Foods & Things to Avoid

  • Moldy hay
  • Sudden grain increase
  • Wild cherry, rhododendron
  • Lone housing

💡 Pro Tips

  • Goats are herd animals; keep at least two.
  • Fence must be tall and strong.
