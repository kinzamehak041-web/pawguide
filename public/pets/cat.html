Cat Care Guide

Independent but affectionate pets that are clean, playful and good for apartments.

DifficultyEasy-Medium
Lifespan12-18 years
CostMedium
SizeSmall

🍽️ Food & Diet

  • Cats are obligate carnivores: feed high-protein cat food (wet food plus dry).
  • Kittens eat 3-4 times a day; adults 2 times.
  • Always keep fresh water; many cats prefer a fountain or wide bowl.
  • Cooked chicken or fish in small amounts is fine; cow milk can upset their stomach.

🏠 Housing

  • A clean litter box in a quiet spot, scooped daily and fully washed weekly.
  • A scratching post, a high perch and a cozy bed.
  • Keep windows and balconies safe with nets.

📅 Daily Routine

  • 15-30 minutes of play with a wand toy or ball.
  • Scoop the litter, refresh water, feed meals.
  • Quiet time and gentle petting.

🛁 Grooming

  • Brush weekly (daily for long-haired cats).
  • Trim nails every 2-3 weeks.
  • Cats rarely need baths; wipe with a damp cloth if dirty.
  • Dental care: brush or use dental treats.

🩺 Health & Vet Care

  • Kitten vaccines, then yearly boosters; rabies vaccine too.
  • Deworm and use flea control.
  • Spay or neuter around 5-6 months unless breeding.
  • Warning signs: hiding for long, straining in the litter box, not eating, constant vomiting.

🎓 Training

  • Place the kitten in the litter box after meals; they learn fast.
  • Redirect scratching to a post with treats.
  • Use clicker or treats for simple tricks and carrier comfort.

⚠️ Foods & Things to Avoid

  • Onion and garlic
  • Chocolate
  • Lilies (extremely toxic)
  • Raw fish only diet
  • Tuna-only diet
  • Human painkillers

💡 Pro Tips

  • Indoor cats live longer.
  • Introduce new pets slowly.
  • Give each cat its own litter box (plus one extra).
