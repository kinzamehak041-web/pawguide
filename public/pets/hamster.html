Hamster Care Guide

Tiny, nocturnal pets that are easy to keep but need a big cage and a wheel.

DifficultyEasy
Lifespan2-3 years
CostLow
SizeTiny

🍽️ Food & Diet

  • Hamster seed mix or pellets (1-2 tablespoons daily).
  • Small pieces of fresh veggies: carrot, cucumber, broccoli.
  • Occasional boiled egg or mealworm for protein.
  • Fresh water in a drip bottle.

🏠 Housing

  • Large cage with deep bedding for burrowing (paper bedding, not cedar).
  • A solid-surface exercise wheel (at least 20 cm for dwarf, 28 cm for Syrian).
  • House solitary Syrian hamsters alone.

📅 Daily Routine

  • Evening play and feeding as they wake.
  • Remove spoiled food from their hidden stash.
  • Spot clean weekly.

🛁 Grooming

  • Offer a sand bath; never water.
  • Nails wear down naturally on wheel and toys.

🩺 Health & Vet Care

  • Wet tail (diarrhea), overgrown teeth, weight loss and lethargy need a vet.
  • Keep temperature stable, 18-24 C.

🎓 Training

  • Let them sniff your hand first and handle gently over a soft surface.
  • Use treats to build trust.

⚠️ Foods & Things to Avoid

  • Citrus
  • Chocolate
  • Onion and garlic
  • Sticky foods
  • Cedar or pine bedding

💡 Pro Tips

  • Never wake a sleeping hamster suddenly.
  • Hamsters can bite when frightened.
