Rabbit Care Guide

Gentle, social herbivores that need lots of hay, space to hop and a calm home.

DifficultyMedium
Lifespan8-12 years
CostLow-Medium
SizeSmall-Medium

🍽️ Food & Diet

  • 80% unlimited fresh hay (timothy or grass hay) - essential for teeth and digestion.
  • A handful of leafy greens daily (coriander, romaine, mint).
  • Small portion of rabbit pellets; carrots and fruit only as rare treats.
  • Fresh water in a heavy bowl or bottle.

🏠 Housing

  • Large pen or hutch at least 4x the rabbit's length, plus daily time outside it.
  • Keep cool: rabbits overheat above 28 C. Avoid direct sun.
  • Soft flooring (not wire) and a hiding box.

📅 Daily Routine

  • 3-4 hours of free roaming in a safe area.
  • Fresh hay and greens twice a day.
  • Spot clean the toilet corner, full clean weekly.

🛁 Grooming

  • Brush weekly, daily when molting.
  • Trim nails every 4-6 weeks.
  • Never bathe in water; spot clean only.

🩺 Health & Vet Care

  • Teeth grow constantly; hay and chew sticks keep them worn.
  • Annual vet check and vaccines where available.
  • Warning signs: no droppings for 12 hours, not eating, head tilt, wet chin. These are emergencies.

🎓 Training

  • Rabbits can learn litter training and their name.
  • Be patient; let them come to you rather than picking them up suddenly.
  • Support the back legs when lifting.

⚠️ Foods & Things to Avoid

  • Iceberg lettuce
  • Chocolate
  • Bread and biscuits
  • Potato leaves
  • Avocado
  • Sudden loud noises

💡 Pro Tips

  • Rabbits are happier in bonded pairs.
  • Bunny-proof wires.
  • They hide pain well, so watch their behavior.
