Ferret Care Guide

Playful, curious carnivores that need high-protein food and plenty of out-of-cage time.

DifficultyHard
Lifespan6-10 years
CostMedium-High
SizeSmall

🍽️ Food & Diet

  • High-quality ferret food or a meat-based diet with high protein and fat.
  • No fruit, sugar or vegetables in large amounts.

🏠 Housing

  • Multi-level cage with hammocks and a litter corner.
  • At least 4 hours out of the cage daily in a ferret-proofed room.

📅 Daily Routine

  • Play, feeding twice or more, litter cleaning.

🛁 Grooming

  • Bathe rarely (every 1-3 months).
  • Trim nails every 2 weeks and clean ears.

🩺 Health & Vet Care

  • Annual vaccines and vet check; ferrets are prone to adrenal disease.
  • Keep cool; they overheat easily.

🎓 Training

  • Litter training works with patience; use treats for tricks.

⚠️ Foods & Things to Avoid

  • Sugary food
  • Dairy
  • Chocolate
  • Small rubber items they might swallow

💡 Pro Tips

  • Check local laws.
  • Ferrets sleep up to 18 hours a day.
