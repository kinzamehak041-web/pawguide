Guinea Pig Care Guide

Gentle, chatty small pets that need vitamin C, hay and a friend.

DifficultyEasy-Medium
Lifespan5-8 years
CostLow-Medium
SizeSmall

🍽️ Food & Diet

  • Unlimited hay daily.
  • Vitamin C-rich vegetables: bell pepper, parsley, kale.
  • Small amount of guinea pig pellets with added vitamin C.
  • Fresh water always.

🏠 Housing

  • Large cage or pen, at least 0.7 square metres for two animals.
  • Soft bedding and a hide house.
  • Keep away from heat and drafts.

📅 Daily Routine

  • Fresh veggies and hay twice a day.
  • An hour of floor time.
  • Spot clean daily; full clean weekly.

🛁 Grooming

  • Brush long-haired breeds daily.
  • Trim nails monthly.
  • Bathe only when needed with guinea pig safe shampoo.

🩺 Health & Vet Care

  • They cannot make vitamin C; deficiency causes scurvy.
  • Watch for sneezing, weight loss, overgrown teeth.
  • Annual vet check by an exotic-animal vet.

🎓 Training

  • Hand-feed veggies to build trust.
  • Talk gently; they respond to routine.

⚠️ Foods & Things to Avoid

  • Iceberg lettuce
  • Chocolate
  • Onion
  • Seeds and nuts
  • Rabbit food

💡 Pro Tips

  • Keep them in pairs.
  • Hay is not optional.
