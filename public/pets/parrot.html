Parrot Care Guide

Intelligent, social birds that need a big cage, mental stimulation and daily interaction.

DifficultyMedium-Hard
Lifespan5-60 years (species dependent)
CostMedium
SizeSmall to large

🍽️ Food & Diet

  • Quality seed and pellet mix as the base.
  • Fresh vegetables and fruit daily (carrot, spinach, apple without seeds).
  • Cuttlebone or mineral block for calcium.
  • Fresh water changed every day.

🏠 Housing

  • Cage wide enough for the bird to fully spread wings and climb.
  • Place at eye level in a bright room away from kitchen fumes.
  • Perches of different thickness, toys and a bath dish.
  • Cover at night for 10-12 hours of sleep.

📅 Daily Routine

  • 1-2 hours out of the cage in a safe room.
  • Talk, whistle and play with them.
  • Remove old food; wash bowls.

🛁 Grooming

  • Offer a shallow bath or light misting weekly.
  • Nails and beak usually stay healthy with perches; a vet can trim if needed.
  • Feathers and wings should only be clipped by a professional.

🩺 Health & Vet Care

  • Fluffed feathers, sitting at the bottom, discharge from eyes or tail bobbing are warning signs.
  • Annual avian vet check.
  • Keep away from Teflon fumes, smoke and strong sprays; these can kill birds.

🎓 Training

  • Build trust first: sit near the cage and talk softly.
  • Step-up training with a treat.
  • Repeat words clearly; reward with attention.

⚠️ Foods & Things to Avoid

  • Avocado
  • Chocolate
  • Caffeine
  • Salt
  • Apple seeds
  • Non-stick cookware fumes

💡 Pro Tips

  • Birds need company; lonely parrots get stressed.
  • Rotate toys to avoid boredom.
  • Never leave in direct hot sun.
