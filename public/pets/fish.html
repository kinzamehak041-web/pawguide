Fish Care Guide

Calm, beautiful pets; success depends on clean, stable water.

DifficultyEasy-Medium
Lifespan2-15 years (species dependent)
CostLow-Medium
SizeSmall

🍽️ Food & Diet

  • Feed good flake or pellet food made for your species.
  • Feed only what they finish in 2 minutes, once or twice a day.
  • Overfeeding is the number one cause of dirty tanks.

🏠 Housing

  • Bigger tanks are more stable: goldfish need 75 L+ each, betta 20 L+.
  • Use a filter, heater for tropical fish and dechlorinated water.
  • Cycle the tank (grow beneficial bacteria) for 4-6 weeks before adding fish.

📅 Daily Routine

  • Feed and check fish look active.
  • Check temperature and that the filter works.

🛁 Grooming

  • Change 20-30% of the water weekly.
  • Rinse filter media in old tank water, not tap water.
  • Wipe algae from glass.

🩺 Health & Vet Care

  • White spots, torn fins, gasping at the surface or clamped fins mean poor water or disease.
  • Test ammonia, nitrite and nitrate regularly.
  • Quarantine new fish for 2 weeks.

🎓 Training

  • Fish learn feeding time and may eat from your hand.

⚠️ Foods & Things to Avoid

  • Tap water without conditioner
  • Overstocking
  • Soap near the tank
  • Mixing aggressive species

💡 Pro Tips

  • Bettas live alone.
  • Never dump all water at once.
