Chicken Care Guide

Productive backyard birds that give eggs and need a safe coop and daily feed.

DifficultyEasy-Medium
Lifespan5-8 years
CostLow
SizeMedium

🍽️ Food & Diet

  • Layer feed with calcium for hens, chick starter for chicks.
  • Kitchen scraps (veggies, grains) in moderation; clean water always.
  • Provide grit and oyster shell.

🏠 Housing

  • Predator-proof coop with 0.4 sq m per bird indoors and run space outside.
  • Perches, nest boxes (1 per 4 hens) and good ventilation.
  • Dry bedding such as straw or wood shavings.

📅 Daily Routine

  • Let out in the morning, collect eggs, check water and feed.
  • Lock the coop at dusk.

🛁 Grooming

  • Provide a dust bath area.
  • Check for mites and lice monthly.

🩺 Health & Vet Care

  • Vaccinate against common poultry diseases (Newcastle, Marek's).
  • Deworm regularly.
  • Isolate any sick bird immediately.

🎓 Training

  • Hens learn to come when called with treats.

⚠️ Foods & Things to Avoid

  • Avocado
  • Chocolate
  • Raw dry beans
  • Moldy food
  • Onion in large amounts

💡 Pro Tips

  • Keep feed in sealed containers to avoid rodents.
  • Clean the coop weekly.
